Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bombshell Boss Babes?

An exclusive division of IAOTP celebrating exceptional women in business, leadership, and entrepreneurship through luxury events, global recognition, and curated media opportunities.

How do I become a member?

By invitation or selection through IAOTP, or by applying via our contact page for consideration.

What packages are available?

Diamond ($5,999 / 5 years), Gold ($3,999 / 3 years), and Crystal ($1,999 / 1 year). See packages for full details.

How does merch shipping work?

Items are printed on demand and dropshipped directly to your door after checkout. See SETUP.md for Printful integration.

Are events open to non-members?

Select events may offer limited guest access; members enjoy VIP access and priority invitations.

Where are events held?

New York, Las Vegas, Miami/Fort Lauderdale, Toronto, and more destinations added each year.

How is BBB connected to IAOTP?

Bombshell Boss Babes operates as a division of IAOTP, highlighting female leaders within that global professional network.
